\subsection*{Implementation and historical provenance}


The handset front end estimates pose from acceleration and angular
velocity, then regresses segment displacement using 148 signal-derived
features. The frozen phone activity model is a 400-tree random forest,
and the watch supplies cabinet events through its three-axis
acceleration channel. Furniture sensors and reference object identities
are excluded from deployable inference. The carrying-to-body mapping,
furniture inventory and category-specific contact semantics remain
declared protocol assumptions.



The 33/108 changed outcomes refer to two earlier single-realisation
grids, legacy versus prefix-consistent generation, including eight
changes at zero added severity. Those historical outcomes are separate
from the present 324-episode replicated matrix. Some earlier caches
retain aggregate scores without every box and match, requiring raw-input
replay for box-level verification.



The original path summaries 2.94 and 1.03 use different available-path
subsets and different summary statistics, so they are retained as
historical diagnostics. Physical synchronisation in the real case
remains unverified. Code availability, model-weight access and raw-data
access require separate descriptions in the final release package.
